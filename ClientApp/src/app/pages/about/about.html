Our Story

A family tradition of liquid gold, passed down through four generations.

Rooted in the Greek Countryside

Our journey began in 1952, when Giorgos Sofianos planted the first olive trees on a sun-drenched hillside in the Peloponnese. What started as a small family farm has grown into a celebrated grove spanning over 200 acres.

Every autumn, we hand-pick our Koroneiki and Kalamata olives at the peak of ripeness. Within hours of harvest, they are cold-pressed to preserve their exceptional flavour, aroma, and nutritional properties.

We believe that great olive oil starts in the ground — in healthy soil, nurtured organically, kissed by the Greek sun and cooled by the Aegean breeze.
